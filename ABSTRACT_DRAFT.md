## Template

### Problem (2 sentences)

Smart contracts control billions in user value, yet their security validation remains challenging. Existing approaches—expert audits, automated testing, or AI-based analysis—each have blind spots; no single method sufficiently validates complex systems.

### Approach (2 sentences)

This research develops a systematic methodology for integrating AI-generated risk assessments with independent deterministic evidence using Dempster-Shafer evidence fusion. The approach was applied to three major DeFi protocols (Aave, Venus, and Compound), validating 22 distinct risk findings through source-code analysis, live contract queries, and behavioral Foundry testing.

### Findings (2 sentences)

Across the three protocols, deterministic evidence largely supported AI reasoning, with high confidence on tested findings. The single case of genuine disagreement (Aave F05) revealed how conflict between evidence sources is informative rather than problematic, pointing to real constraints on risk rather than AI failure.

### Implications (1 sentence)

The framework provides validators with a transparent, decision-support tool for combining evidence sources, with explicit tracking of what has been tested, what remains uncertain, and where independent evidence sources agree or diverge.
